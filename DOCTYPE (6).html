01

Elves & sea-songs

Beside the fires

Siron

Man ti?Who are they?

Amdiron

In edhil ennas.The elves there.

Siron

In yn Elrond.The sons of Elrond.

Amdiron

In eneth dîn Elladan a Elrohir.Their names are Elladan and Elrohir.

Siron

I chent dîn laeg!Their eyes are keen!

Amdiron

Navan Ivrinel?Where is Ivrinel? Literally, “At what place is Ivrinel?”

Siron

Ganna aerlinn nef i noer.She’s harping sea-songs beside the fires.

04

The special case of AW

One syllable: AW remains AW

In a single-syllable word, AW is pronounced identically to AU and is treated as a diphthong. Just as AU remains AU, AW remains AW.

Raw → Raw*Lion → lions
Naw → Naw*Idea → ideas

More than one syllable: treat A separately

In longer words, AW is treated as two separate sounds. Pluralise the A according to the rules in Lesson 6.

Awarth → EwerthAbandonment → abandonments
Tawar → TewairMaterial wood / a forest → some wood / forests

*Unchanged plurals supplied from the source’s stated AW rule.
