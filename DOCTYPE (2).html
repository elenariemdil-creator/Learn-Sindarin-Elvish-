01

Where are you from?

Being able to say where you come from is an important part of a first conversation with a stranger. Amdiron and Ivrinel are still relative strangers, so they continue to use formal speech.

This lesson is covered in more detail in Weeks 1 and 7 of Introduction to Sindarin, as noted on the original page.

A place to call home

Amdiron

Suilad! Navan dorthol Ivrinel?Greetings! Where do you live, Ivrinel?

Ivrinel

Dorthon vi Lothlorien, ach onnen hí vi Imladris. A len?I live in Lothlorien, but I was born here in Rivendell. And you?

Amdiron

Telin od Imladris.I come from Rivendell.

Ivrinel

Oman tôl?Where does he come from?

Amdiron

Siron? Mellonenin tôl o Daur-nu-Fuin.Siron? My friend comes from Mirkwood.

02

Vocabulary

Start with these phrases. For now, you can use them as building blocks without learning all of the grammar behind them.

Suilad!Greetings!
Navan dorthol?Where do you live?Formal speech.
Dorthon vi…I live in…
achBut.
onnen híI was born here.
A len?And you?Formal speech.
Telin o(d)…I come from…Use o before a consonant and od before a vowel.
Oman tôl?Where does he come from?

O or od?

The word for “from” changes according to the sound that follows it. Notice the two forms in the dialogue.

Before a consonant

o Daur-nu-FuinFrom Mirkwood.

Before a vowel

od ImladrisFrom Rivendell.

03

I live, you live, we live

To say “we live in,” use Dortham vi followed by a place. Notice how the ending differs from Dorthon and Dorthol.

Dorthon vi…I live in…
Dorthol vi…You live in… (formal)
Dortham vi…We live in…
Notice, rather than memorise the grammar. Verbs are covered in a later lesson. For now, focus on recognising the different endings.
04

Formal vs informal

Use formal speech with a stranger or authority figure. With someone you know well, use the informal version of the question.

Formal

Navan dorthol?To a stranger or authority figure.

Informal

Navan dorthog?To someone you know well.

Both questions mean “Where do you live?”

05

Places in the dialogue

These are the place names used in Amdiron and Ivrinel’s conversation.

LothlorienWhere Ivrinel lives.
ImladrisRivendell.Ivrinel’s birthplace and the place Amdiron comes from.
Daur-nu-FuinMirkwood.The place Siron comes from in this dialogue.
Try it yourself. Practise asking Navan dorthol? and replying with Dorthon vi… Then practise Telin o(d)…, choosing the correct form of “from.”
